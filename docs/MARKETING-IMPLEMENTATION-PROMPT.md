# Reg Mitra public-site implementation prompt

Implement the approved Reg Mitra positioning, public-site copy, product-language
truth pass, and marketing visuals.

The canonical source of truth is:

`docs/MARKETING-MESSAGING-AND-VISUAL-EXECUTION-BRIEF.md`

Read that file completely before editing. Use its recommended copy exactly.
Do not shorten, “improve”, or strengthen product claims.

## Objective

Make the public experience communicate this chain within three seconds:

**Official source → client applicability review → internal draft → professional
approval**

The homepage headline must be:

**Turn regulatory updates into review-ready client work.**

The public CTAs must be:

- **Explore the sample workspace**
- **Request pilot access**

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- Do not claim comprehensive or continuous regulatory discovery.
- Do not claim automatic professional client-impact conclusions.
- Do not imply that approval enables Reg Mitra to send, file, pay, submit, or
  change an external system.
- Do not use a generic `Verified` label.
- Keep source link, full-text indexing, URL reachability, applicability review,
  internal approval, and externally confirmed completion as separate states.
- Do not invent prices, testimonials, customer logos, usage metrics, or
  performance claims.
- Do not introduce shields, locks, gavels, floating papers, robots, AI
  gradients, stock photography, decorative glows, or unreadable screenshots.
- Preserve unrelated work already present in the worktree.
